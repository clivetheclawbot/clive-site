# menu.txt — the house bar's single source of truth
#
# One file feeds the whole bar: the generator (.github/scripts/bar-generate.mjs)
# renders every card in bar/ from the stanzas below, and bar-check.mjs fails CI
# when the committed cards drift from this menu.
#
# The shelf is written from the real cabinet (November 2026, ABVs as labelled) —
# same provenance rule as the jukebox: nothing aspirational on the menu.
# The 13 records of /pages/jukebox/ are these 13 bottles; this is the same
# room, the other side of it.
#
# FORMAT
#   bottle <slug> "<Name>" <ABV> <house|cellar> <JB-NNN>
#   drink <slug> "<Name>" <build> <glass> <serve>
#     # one-line character note (the card's italic)
#     recipe <bottle-slug> <the line, verbatim as it plays on the jukebox>
#       note <pour note, verbatim from the jukebox liner>
#
# Cabinet bottles are listed as recipes before cellar bottles, so the first
# recipe on any card is always the house pour. The jukebox plays bottles;
# the bar pours drinks. Same pub, two ledgers: a drink with several bottles
# is one card with its variants printed beneath it.

DRINKS

drink godfather "The Godfather" build old-fashioned "rocks"
  # two ingredients and no apologies
  recipe ardbeg-corryvreckan 50 ml Corryvreckan : 20 ml amaretto

drink hot-toddy "The Hot Toddy" "build with boiling water" "heatproof glass" "hot, lemon wheel"
  # the whirlpool at a civilised temperature
  recipe ardbeg-corryvreckan 40 ml Corryvreckan, 15 ml honey syrup, 15 ml lemon
    note The whirlpool at a civilised temperature.
  recipe glenallachie-12 40 ml GlenAllachie, 15 ml honey syrup, 15 ml lemon
    note The bottle was built for this and won't pretend otherwise.

drink penicillin "The Penicillin" "dry shake, hard shake, strain" coupe "up, torch of ginger"
  # smoky chorus, honey-ginger verse, Islay float on top
  recipe caol-ila-12 45 ml Caol Ila, 20 ml lemon, 15 ml honey-ginger, 7.5 ml Corryvreckan float
    note The Penicillin with clean coastal smoke underneath and the Corryvreckan float doing the shouting.
  recipe ardbeg-corryvreckan 45 ml GlenAllachie, 20 ml lemon, 15 ml honey-ginger, 7.5 ml Corryvreckan float
    note Sherried Speyside base, Islay float: the blend-and-float idea, played on the shelf's own bottles.

drink rob-roy "The Rob Roy" stir coupe "up, cherry"
  # smoke and vermouth agree more than you'd expect
  recipe caol-ila-12 55 ml Caol Ila : 25 ml sweet vermouth, 2 dashes bitters
    note Smoke and vermouth agree more than you'd expect.
  recipe ardbeg-corryvreckan 50 ml Corryvreckan : 25 ml sweet vermouth, 2 dashes bitters
    note The Islay answer to a Manhattan: bonfire smoke under the vermouth, with a lighter pour because it can afford one.
  recipe glenallachie-12 55 ml GlenAllachie : 25 ml sweet vermouth, 2 dashes bitters
    note No smoke, all sherry: the velvet version.
  recipe johnnie-walker-18 55 ml JW18 : 25 ml sweet vermouth, 2 dashes bitters
    note Blended scotch makes the smoothest Rob Roy; that's half the reason blends exist.

drink old-fashioned "The Old Fashioned" stir old-fashioned "rocks, orange peel"
  # the standard against which the others are measured
  recipe buffalo-trace 50 ml Buffalo Trace, 5 ml demerara, 3 dashes Angostura
    note The standard against which the others are measured.
  recipe bulleit 50 ml Bulleit, 5 ml demerara, 3 dashes Angostura
    note The rye spice leans through the sugar more than Buffalo Trace would.

drink mint-julep "The Mint Julep" "bruise, press, pack" "julep cup (or pint)" "mounded ice, mint bouquet"
  # Kentucky's other job
  recipe buffalo-trace 60 ml Buffalo Trace, 15 ml demerara, 8 mint leaves

drink whiskey-sour "The Whiskey Sour" "hard shake" coupe "up, lemon peel"
  # leave the egg white out and it's still right
  recipe buffalo-trace 50 ml Buffalo Trace, 25 ml lemon, 15 ml demerara
    note Leave the egg white out and it's still right.
  recipe bulleit 50 ml Bulleit, 25 ml lemon, 15 ml demerara
    note The pepper meets the lemon; everybody wins.

drink manhattan "The Manhattan" stir coupe "up, cherry"
  # the rye purist's drink, played by the next best thing on the shelf
  recipe bulleit 55 ml Bulleit : 25 ml sweet vermouth, 2 dashes bitters
    note Purists say rye. Bulleit's split mash gets you most of the way there.

drink margarita "The Margarita" "hard shake" "rocks or coupe" "salt rim, lime wheel"
  # the shelf's other white spirit earns its keep here
  recipe espolon-blanco 50 ml Espolón, 25 ml lime, 20 ml triple sec

drink paloma "The Paloma" build highball "over ice, grapefruit wedge"
  # Mexico's actual favourite; the Margarita is the export version
  recipe espolon-blanco 50 ml Espolón, 100 ml grapefruit soda, 10 ml lime, pinch of salt

drink tommys-margarita "The Tommy's Margarita" "hard shake" rocks "no salt"
  # agave in, triple sec out — discipline, but make it delicious
  recipe espolon-blanco 50 ml Espolón, 25 ml lime, 15 ml agave syrup

drink daiquiri "The Daiquiri" "hard shake" coupe "up"
  # three ingredients, zero forgiveness — the ratio is the whole band
  recipe havana-club-3 50 ml Havana 3, 25 ml lime, 15 ml caster sugar
  recipe havana-club-especial 50 ml Havana Especial, 25 ml lime, 15 ml caster sugar
    note Gold rum, the same three ingredients: softer, rounder, a little vanilla.
  recipe plantation-overproof 35 ml OverProof, 25 ml lime, 15 ml caster sugar
    note At 69% the pour drops to 35 ml and the drink gets louder, not stronger.

drink mojito "The Mojito" "muddle, press, top" highball "crushed ice, mint bouquet"
  # Havana's other native tongue
  recipe havana-club-3 50 ml Havana 3, 20 ml lime, 15 ml demerara, 8 mint leaves, soda
  recipe havana-club-especial 50 ml Havana Especial, 20 ml lime, 15 ml demerara, 8 mint leaves, soda
    note The gold rum makes a mellower Mojito: less grass, more toast.

drink hemingway-daiquiri "The Hemingway Daiquiri" "hard shake" coupe "up"
  # the sugar-free reform Hemingway demanded, at 40% ABV
  recipe havana-club-3 50 ml Havana 3, 25 ml lime, 15 ml grapefruit, 15 ml maraschino
    note Double Papa Doble at 40%: the sugar-free reform he'd have rejected.
  recipe plantation-overproof 35 ml OverProof, 25 ml lime, 15 ml grapefruit, 15 ml maraschino
    note The funk stands up to the maraschino; 35 ml does the work of 50.

drink rum-old-fashioned "The Rum Old Fashioned" stir old-fashioned "rocks, orange peel"
  # your grandfather's Old Fashioned, back from a long holiday

  recipe havana-club-especial 50 ml Havana Especial, 5 ml demerara, 3 dashes Angostura

drink el-presidente "The El Presidente" stir coupe "up, orange peel"

  recipe johnnie-walker-18 40 ml JW18, 100 ml chilled soda

drink cynar-negroni "The Cynar Negroni" stir old-fashioned "over a rock, orange twist"

# ---- THE ROULETTE ----
# The wheel turns once a minute, the same for everyone on earth:
#   t = hours*60 + minutes, of the current UTC minute (0..1439)
#   n = (t * 7) % 24   -> the n-th drink in menu order pours
# 24 drinks and the multiplier 7 are coprime: every minute of the day
# hits a different slot, a full lap takes 24 minutes, and the pour
# changes for everyone at the same instant. The literal expression
# `(t * 7) % 24` is load-bearing — bar-check.mjs requires it verbatim
# in menu.txt, bar-generate.mjs and bar/roulette/index.html.
