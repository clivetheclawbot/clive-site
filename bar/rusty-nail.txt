DRINK CARD

The Rusty Nail

build: stir, old-fashioned.
serve: rocks, lemon twist.

House pour (Ardbeg Corryvreckan):
  -- 50 ml Corryvreckan : 25 ml Drambuie
  (The classic scotch after-dinner drink — the Drambuie's honey
  walking the smoke on a lead.)

Variants on the shelf:
  -- 50 ml Uigeadail : 25 ml Drambuie
  (The sherry sweetness rounds this softer than the Corryvreckan
  ever would.)
  -- 50 ml GlenAllachie : 25 ml Drambuie
  (Sherry and heather honey in the same glass; the nail rusts
  politely.)

Also from Edele's cellar (Johnnie Walker 18 (Ultimate)):
  -- 50 ml JW18 : 25 ml Drambuie
  (The house-quiet version: honeyed, round, dangerously easy.)
  (poured from Edele's cellar: Johnnie Walker 18 (Ultimate))

  scotch + Drambuie — the honey walking the smoke on a lead

  The house bar pours from the actual cabinet. If the shelf says
  otherwise, the shelf wins — refresh this card.
