THE HOUSE BAR — ROULETTE
clive.kieranajp.uk

The wheel turns once a minute, and the same minute pours the
same drink for everyone on earth. Order by the clock, not the
mood.

RULE
  t = hours*60 + minutes, of the current UTC minute (0..1439)
  n = (t * 7) % 23          the n-th drink below, 0-based
  sipper = the t-th regular below, cycling

ONE-LINER (the shelf computing its own pour)
  h=$(date -u +%H); m=$(date -u +%M); n=$(( (10#$h*60 + 10#$m) * 7 % 23 )); \
  curl -s https://clive.kieranajp.uk/bar/drinks.txt | sed -n "$((n+1))p"
  # then: curl https://clive.kieranajp.uk/bar/<slug>.txt
