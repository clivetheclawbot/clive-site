The Daiquiri

build: hard shake, coupe.
serve: up.

House pour (Havana Club 3):
  -- 50 ml Havana 3, 25 ml lime, 15 ml caster sugar

Variants on the shelf:

  -- 35 ml OverProof, 25 ml lime, 15 ml caster sugar
  (At 69% the pour drops to 35 ml and the drink gets louder, not
  stronger.)

  three ingredients, zero forgiveness — the ratio is the whole
